BBuyWise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

Summary: BuyWise runs entirely in your browser. We operate no servers, collect no personal data, and use no analytics or tracking. Your API keys and history stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. BuyWise has no backend. We do not receive, store, or transmit any of your data to us, and we do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

Data stored on your device

The following is saved locally via the browser's chrome.storage and never leaves your device except as described below:

  • API keys you enter (OpenAI; optional YouTube and Perplexity);
  • Preferences (region, AI model, budget, preferred brands, toggles);
  • Recent searches, a 24-hour cache of verdicts, and a self-built price history of products you view.

You can erase all of it at any time by removing the extension or clearing its storage.

Data sent to third parties (only the ones you enable)

To produce a verdict, BuyWise sends your product query and gathered context directly from your browser to the services you have configured — never through us:

  • OpenAI (required): your query and review context, using your own API key.
  • Reddit (public, no key): product discussion lookups.
  • YouTube Data API (optional, your key): video reviews/comments.
  • Perplexity (optional, your key): additional web research in Deep mode.

Each provider processes that data under its own privacy policy. Your keys are sent only to their respective provider for authentication and are never transmitted to BuyWise.

Product pages you visit

On supported store pages (Amazon, Flipkart), BuyWise reads publicly visible product information (title, price, rating, and shown reviews) in your browser to ground the verdict. It never logs into, or accesses the account on, any store. This information may be included in the prompt sent to your chosen AI provider as part of an analysis you request.

Children

BuyWise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
